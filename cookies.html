Cookies

There is no cookie banner on this site because there are no cookies to tell you about.

Last updated 2 November 2026.

We do not use cookies

This website sets no cookies of any kind: no essential cookies, no analytics cookies, no advertising cookies. There is nothing to consent to and nothing to opt out of.

What the site does load

  • The pages, the stylesheet, the font and the pictures, all served from this domain, and one small script that moves the ring on the home page and animates the page headings.

Nothing else is fetched from anywhere else. There are no third-party embeds, no advertising, no analytics and no fingerprinting.

Nothing is kept in your browser

The site stores nothing in your browser: no cookies, and no other local storage.

The Join the community link leads to a separate site, which has its own rules about cookies and privacy.

The desktop

Trindle is a desktop, not a website, and uses no cookies. What it keeps on your own computer, and none of it leaves, is described on the privacy page.

Apps you install yourself are their own affair: a web browser from Apps will use cookies exactly as it would on any other computer.
