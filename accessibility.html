Accessibility

This site is intended to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.2 at level AA. Here is what has been done and what has not.

What we have done

  • Every page works from the keyboard alone, and the focus outline is visible against the background at every step.
  • Text and background colours were measured for contrast rather than eyeballed. The site has one dark theme.
  • Headings are in order, landmarks are marked up, and each page starts with a skip link.
  • Links and buttons have touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, including the menu button and every row in the menu it opens.
  • Pictures carry text alternatives; the drawings that only decorate a point are hidden from screen readers.
  • The layout reflows to a narrow screen without horizontal scrolling, down to 320 pixels wide.
  • Nothing flashes. Moving parts of the page can be paused with a button, and every bit of movement stops when your system asks for reduced motion.
  • The site works with JavaScript switched off, including the menu.

Known limitations

Some panels carry a good deal of text. If anything is hard to read, your browser's own zoom works throughout.

Trindle itself

The desktop is mouse-first on purpose. Everything can be done by pointing, nothing has to be memorised, and the ring puts the whole desktop within reach of one hand on a mouse or a trackball, which suits some people far better than a keyboard-driven desktop does.

That cuts the other way too. Someone who cannot use a pointer at all is not well served yet: there are keyboard shortcuts, but they are a convenience rather than a complete route through everything. The desktop has not been formally audited against a desktop accessibility standard, and it has no screen reader of its own yet. Its windows describe themselves to a screen reader through the standard accessibility link, but none is installed, and Trindle’s own is being built. The ring has a magnifier button, and an on-screen keyboard button once it is switched on in Settings.

Telling us about a problem

If something on this site or in the desktop shuts you out, we want to know, and we will fix it. Write to distro@trindle.org and say what you were trying to do and what got in the way.

This statement was reviewed on 6 November 2026. It rests on our own checks: every page's markup, and every width from 320 to 1400 pixels. The site has not yet been tested with a screen reader by a person.
