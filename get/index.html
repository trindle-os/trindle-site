Download Trindle

Trindle is in beta, version 0.9.0-beta.1. One .iso file; write it to a USB stick and try it before you change anything on the disk.

Check your download

The image comes with two more files next to it.

SHA256SUMSThe SHA-256 of the image under its two names, trindle-amd64.iso and trindle-0.9.0-beta.1-amd64.iso, one line each, in the form <hash> <file name>. Both files are the same image.
SHA256SUMS.sigA signature of that file, made with the Trindle Archive key.
Key fingerprintDC23 9DC3 4F02 A6A6 D569 E83B 6D17 DFB7 011D D6AA

The same key signs Trindle’s updates. Its public half is in system/apt/trindle-archive-keyring.gpg in the source, and on an installed Trindle at /usr/share/keyrings/trindle-archive-keyring.gpg.

Put the three files in one folder, then:

gpg --import trindle-archive-keyring.gpg
gpg --fingerprint DC239DC34F02A6A6D569E83B6D17DFB7011DD6AA
gpg --verify SHA256SUMS.sig SHA256SUMS
sha256sum -c SHA256SUMS --ignore-missing

Compare the fingerprint that gpg --fingerprint prints with the one above, character by character. gpg --verify must say Good signature, and sha256sum -c must say trindle-amd64.iso: OK. If either says otherwise, do not use the image.
