Complaints

If something we have made has let you down, tell us and we will put it right or explain why we cannot.

Last updated 3 October 2026.

Tell us

Write to distro@trindle.org. Please tell us what happened, what you expected instead, and how you would like it put right.

What happens next

  • We aim to acknowledge a complaint within five working days.
  • We aim to give a full answer within twenty working days. If it is going to take longer, we will tell you why and give you a date.
  • If we got something wrong, we will say so plainly and tell you what we have changed.

If you are still not satisfied

Say so, and ask for the complaint to be looked at again. We are a small project, so this means it will be reconsidered directly rather than passed through a department.

If your complaint concerns personal data and you are not satisfied with our answer, you may take it to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Bugs are not complaints

If the software is simply not working properly, the fastest route is not this page but the report page, where it can be reproduced, tracked and fixed in the open.

The same goes for a missing feature or a screen whose wording is confusing: both are more useful in the open than in an inbox.

Trindle

No bar, no dock, no menu. A desktop with just one ring, written in Rust and made for a mouse or a touchscreen.

Try to accept our cookies

Spoiler: there isn’t one. We don’t do cookies, so there is nothing to accept.

Your privacy is paramount.
